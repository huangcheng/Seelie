# Seelie character bible (frozen) — sakura pink edition

Reference images: `refs/sakura_preview.png`, `refs/sakura_front.jpg`, `refs/sakura_hd.jpg`

## Identity
- Chibi / Nendoroid-like 3D-cute anime girl, large head, small body
- Soft white bob hair with pastel pink streaks/highlights
- Large pink eyes with heart-shaped highlights; soft blush; tiny smile
- Oversized light-pink hoodie with **hood up**, pointed cat-ear hood, sakura flowers on each ear
- White drawstrings ending in sakura flower toggles; small pink shoulder bows
- Sakura petal prints on sleeves / kangaroo pocket
- Pink pleated skirt with white ruffle underskirt + sakura pattern
- White ankle socks with pink trim; chunky white sneakers with pink laces + sakura accents
- One small sakura flower tucked in hair

## Palette (strict)
- Primary: soft pastel pink, rose pink, clean white
- Accents: sakura blossom pink/white only
- **Banned:** purple, lavender, violet, magenta clothing, cyan crystals, ice-blue trim from the old mascot

## Style
- Clean polished 3D-chibi / collectible-figure look, soft even lighting
- **Chroma green screen (#00FF00)** behind character for lossless PNG matte (not white JPEG)
- **Character only** — no human hands, no walls/ledges/furniture, no props or tools
- **4–8 frames per clip** (walk/idle loops = 8; reactions ≥5; never 3)
- Output: **RGBA PNG** per frame (`output_format: png` from API)
- No text, watermarks, UI, floating icons, extra characters
- Keep outfit and flowers identical across every clip
